Research

My research focuses on building principled and scalable algorithms for generative models and large language models, driven by a core question: how can we design learning algorithms that are simultaneously theoretically grounded, computationally efficient, and practically impactful?

Recently, I have worked on steering flow and diffusion models efficiently, finding the few directions in noise space that matter and developing guidance approaches that are both manifold-preserving and Pareto-aware. Currently, I am exploring one-step diffusion and long-horizon agentic tasks such as RL-trained terminal agents, where efficient generation and decision-making must go hand in hand.

Divide and Learn: Multi-Objective Combinatorial Optimization at Scale
Esha Singh, Dongxia Wu, Chien-Yi Yang, Tajana Rosing, Rose Yu, Yi-An Ma
ICML 2026
Paper Blog Code Poster

Reformulates multi-objective combinatorial optimization as online learning over decomposed decision spaces, achieving 80–98% of specialized solvers with two to three orders of magnitude improvement in sample and computational efficiency.

Learning Where to Steer Noise: Efficient Offline Multi-Objective Optimization with Generative Models
Yuan Lu*, Esha Singh*, Yi-An Ma, Yusu Wang
Preprint, 2026
Paper Blog Code

Steers only the initial noise of a generative model toward the Pareto front, using a few sensitive directions estimated once per task with a Recursive Feature Machine. Outranks the best prior generative method at a fraction of its sampling cost.
